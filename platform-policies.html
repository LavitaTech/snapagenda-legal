Safety & Conduct

Platform Policies

Responsible-use standards for captured content, event creation, calendar sharing, invitations, intellectual property, security, and platform integrity.

Effective September 12, 2026 Lavitatech LLC · SnapAgenda

1. Purpose and scope

These Platform Policies govern use of SnapAgenda's captured content, automated event suggestions, calendar tools, sharing, invitations, and related functionality. They apply in addition to the Terms of Service.

2. Content you submit

Only submit photographs, screenshots, documents, invitations, notes, agendas, images, or other materials that you are legally permitted to process.

Do not use SnapAgenda to process stolen, unlawfully obtained, privacy-invasive, exploitative, malicious, or infringing materials.

3. Privacy and confidential information

Respect the privacy and confidentiality of other people. Do not upload information when doing so violates a legal, professional, contractual, employment, educational, or other confidentiality obligation.

Avoid submitting passwords, authentication codes, financial credentials, or information unrelated to the scheduling task.

4. Calendar and event accuracy

Users must review generated event information before saving, sending, sharing, or relying on it.

SnapAgenda may misunderstand or omit dates, times, time zones, locations, names, instructions, or other information. Automated output should not be treated as independently verified.

5. Invitations and sharing

Calendar invitations and shared agendas must be used for legitimate organizational and scheduling purposes.

Users may not use SnapAgenda for:

  • spam or unsolicited bulk invitations;
  • harassment, stalking, threats, or intimidation;
  • fraudulent or misleading event invitations;
  • phishing or credential collection;
  • impersonation;
  • malicious links or malware distribution;
  • attempts to deceive recipients about an event's organizer, purpose, location, or identity.

6. Intellectual property

Do not submit copyrighted, trademarked, confidential, proprietary, or other protected content unless you have an appropriate legal right to do so.

Users may not copy, scrape, reproduce, decompile, reverse engineer, or misuse SnapAgenda's proprietary software, design, workflows, processing systems, prompts, architecture, branding, or other protected materials except where applicable law expressly permits it.

7. Fraud and malicious activity

SnapAgenda may not be used for fraud, phishing, credential theft, account takeover, impersonation, malware, unauthorized system access, social engineering, deceptive calendar invitations, or other malicious conduct.

8. Illegal and dangerous content

Users may not use the Services to facilitate unlawful activity, exploitation, credible threats, trafficking, non-consensual intimate material, sexual exploitation of minors, or other content prohibited by law.

9. Security and platform integrity

Users may not attempt to bypass access controls, authentication, usage limits, account restrictions, security measures, service protections, or technical safeguards.

Unauthorized probing, scraping, automated abuse, vulnerability exploitation, interference with infrastructure, and attempts to obtain proprietary system information are prohibited.

10. Enforcement

Depending on severity, frequency, context, and risk, SnapAgenda may remove or restrict content, restrict features, suspend or terminate accounts, preserve relevant evidence, investigate suspected abuse, or take other reasonable action.

Where appropriate or required by law, serious suspected illegal activity or credible threats may be referred to appropriate authorities.

11. Reporting and support

Suspected abuse, security issues, or policy violations may be reported to support@lavitatech.com .

Reports should be made in good faith and include enough information for us to understand the concern.

12. Policy updates

We may update these policies as SnapAgenda features, risks, technologies, and legal requirements evolve. The current version will be posted here with its effective date.
