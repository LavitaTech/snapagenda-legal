Support

Contact SnapAgenda for account, privacy, calendar, event, invitation, or technical support.

Effective September 12, 2026 Lavitatech LLC · SnapAgenda

How can we help?

SnapAgenda support can help with account access, profile information, account deletion, privacy requests, calendar permissions, event creation, invitations, and technical issues.

Email SnapAgenda Support

Support email: support@lavitatech.com

Include these details when relevant

  • the email associated with your SnapAgenda account;
  • a short description of the problem;
  • the type of device and operating-system version;
  • the SnapAgenda app version;
  • screenshots that do not expose passwords, authentication codes, payment credentials, or unnecessary sensitive data;
  • whether the issue involves capture, upload, event review, calendar creation, sharing, or another feature.

Event accuracy

If SnapAgenda interprets information incorrectly, review and correct the proposed event before relying on it. For important appointments or deadlines, verify the information against the original source.

Privacy requests

For access, correction, deletion, or other privacy requests, email support@lavitatech.com with the subject “SnapAgenda Privacy Request.”

Security concerns

If you believe your account or SnapAgenda security may have been compromised, contact support promptly and do not send passwords or authentication codes by email.

Legal entity

SnapAgenda is a consumer-facing brand of Lavitatech LLC. Legal notices concerning SnapAgenda should identify Lavitatech LLC as the operator of the SnapAgenda brand.
